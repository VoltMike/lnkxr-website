The problem

Engineering validation is fragmented.

Different tools. Different hardware. Different environments. Engineering knowledge gets trapped between them.

It shouldn't.

The idea

Let engineering flow.

Validation intent is defined once. It runs on SIL, CHIL, HIL and lab hardware through adapters, in your own lab or hosted by LNKXR. Tools and hardware can change. The engineering knowledge stays.
